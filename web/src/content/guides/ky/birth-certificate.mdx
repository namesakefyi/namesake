# Updating Your Kentucky Birth Certificate

This guide covers information from the Kentucky Youth Law Project about changing your name or gender marker on a Kentucky birth certificate.

## Get prepared

The Kentucky Youth Law Project (KYLP) brochure explains that Kentucky birth certificates may be amended to reflect a legal name change and, in some circumstances, a change to the gender marker.

The brochure cites Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) 213.121(4) and (5).

The requirements for updating your name and gender marker are different.

## Change your name on your birth certificate

According to KYLP, you must provide a certified copy of a court order that legally changes your name.

### If you are under 18

The KYLP brochure states that, for minors, the certified court order must be submitted with a request from a parent, guardian, or legal representative.

### If you need a court order

See the Kentucky court-order guide for information about filing a legal name-change petition.

## Change your gender marker

The KYLP materials discuss changing the gender marker on a Kentucky birth certificate.

The brochure titled "Changing Your Gender Marker on Federal and Kentucky IDs" identifies the following documents under its discussion of Kentucky requirements:

- An amended birth certificate displaying the correct gender; or
- A court order that updates your gender marker.

The brochure also explains that Kentucky law contains specific rules for amending birth certificates.

It cites KRS 213.121 and KRS 311.372.

The KYLP name-change brochure advises individuals seeking to update the gender on their birth certificate to speak with an attorney for more detailed information.

**Important:** These are statements from the supplied KYLP brochures, not confirmation of current eligibility or requirements. The applicable law and agency policies must be checked before filing.

## Contact the Office of Vital Statistics

The KYLP brochure identifies the Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics as the agency responsible for Kentucky birth certificate amendments.

It provides the following contact information:

Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics

275 E. Main Street, Suite #1E-A  
Frankfort, KY 40621

Phone: 502-564-4212

The brochure explains that if you were born in another state, you should contact that state's vital statistics office.

Confirm the office's current address, contact information, submission procedures, and fees before applying.

## Update your other documents

After receiving an amended birth certificate, you may need to update your other identification documents.

The KYLP materials discuss updating:

- Kentucky driver's licenses and identification cards
- Social Security records
- Passports

Each agency has its own requirements.

The KYLP brochure recommends checking the current rules of the agency responsible for the document you want to change.
