# How to Legally Change Your Name in Kentucky

This guide explains the legal name change process in Kentucky, including filing a petition, parental consent requirements for minors, court hearings, and updating your records after a name change.

## Get prepared

A legal name change in Kentucky involves filing a petition with the court.

### Adults

According to the Kentucky Youth Law Project (KYLP) brochure, adults do not need permission from their parents to legally change their names.

### Minors

The KYLP brochure states that if you are a minor, both living parents must consent to and sign the Petition for a Name Change.

If only one parent is living, or if the minor is cared for by a guardian, that parent or guardian must sign the petition.

Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) 401.020 addresses name-change petitions involving minors.

See [KRS 401.020](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/Law/statutes/).

## Complete your petition

### Adults

The KYLP brochure recommends filing a petition for a name change through the appropriate county court.

The brochure does not identify a specific adult petition form. Confirm the required form with the court clerk.

### Minors

The KYLP brochure explains that the parent or guardian must sign the Petition for a Name Change.

### If one parent objects

According to KYLP, when one parent agrees to the name change but the other does not, the consenting parent must notify the objecting parent in accordance with the Rules of Civil Procedure.

The objecting parent must receive notice of the upcoming name-change hearing and an opportunity to explain their objection.

The court considers arguments for and against the requested name change.

If the minor is old enough to express an opinion, the judge may ask the minor why the change would be in their best interest.

The court then decides whether granting the name change is in the minor's best interest.

Source: KRS 401.020, as cited in the KYLP brochure.

## File your petition

KYLP recommends filing a name-change petition in the Probate Division of the county's District Court or in the Family Court of the county's Circuit Court.

The brochure advises checking with the court clerk's office about the filing fee because fees vary by county.

Confirm the appropriate court and filing procedure before submitting your petition.

### If you cannot afford the filing fee

The KYLP brochure states that a person who cannot afford court costs may request permission to proceed in forma pauperis using Form AOC-026.

This is a request for the court to waive qualifying fees.

The court must determine whether the request should be granted.

## Attend your hearing

If a hearing is required, the court will consider the petition and any relevant objections.

### If an evidentiary hearing is required

The KYLP brochure cites Iles v. Salisbury, No. 2015-CA-000619-ME (Ky. March 25, 2016), an unpublished decision, for the use of the preponderance-of-the-evidence standard in name-change matters.

The brochure also references Ickes v. Ickes, 793 S.W.2d 118, 122 (Ky. 1990), in discussing the best interests of the child.

These citations should be reviewed before publication to confirm their current applicability.

## Get your court order

If the court grants your petition, obtain the court documentation needed to update your other records.

The KYLP brochure explains that a certified court order documenting a legal name change may be used to request an amendment to a Kentucky birth certificate.

The brochure does not specify the number of certified copies to request or the current cost of obtaining them. Confirm these details with the court clerk.

## Protect your privacy

The Kentucky Youth Law Project recommends requesting that the court seal records associated with a name change.

KYLP explains that name-change proceedings are generally public records and may be accessible online.

The brochure recommends asking the court to seal records in both the court's files and the county clerk's records to help protect privacy and safety.

Sealing is a request to the court and should not be treated as automatic.

## Next steps

After obtaining your name-change order, you may need to update other identification documents.

### Update your birth certificate

According to the KYLP brochure, a Kentucky birth certificate may be amended to reflect a legal name change.

A certified copy of the court order is required for the name amendment described in the brochure.

For minors, the brochure states that the court order must be submitted with a request from a parent, guardian, or legal representative.

See KRS 213.121(4) and (5), as cited in the KYLP brochure.

### Update your Kentucky identification card or driver's license

The Kentucky Driver Licensing Regional Offices handout identifies several categories of documents used for identification applications:

- Proof of identity
- Proof of Social Security number
- Proof of residency
- Proof of any name change, gender change, or other additional documentation, if applicable

The handout identifies a certified legal name-change order as one form of name-change documentation.

Confirm the current document requirements with Kentucky Driver Licensing before applying.
