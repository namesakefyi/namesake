## Get prepared

Kentucky offers standard driver's licenses, permits, identification cards, and REAL IDs.

The Kentucky Driver Licensing Regional Offices handout lists the following documents for applicants:

1. One proof of identity.
2. One proof of Social Security number.
3. Two proofs of Kentucky residency for a REAL ID, or one for a standard credential.
4. Legal proof of a name change or gender change, if applicable.

Original documents or certified copies may be required. Photocopies of identity documents are not accepted.

## Gather proof of identity

The Kentucky Driver Licensing handout lists several acceptable documents.

### Birth certificate

- U.S. birth certificate issued by an appropriate government agency.
- U.S. Consular Report of Birth Abroad.

A hospital-issued birth certificate is not accepted.

### Passport documents

- Valid, unexpired U.S. passport.
- Valid, unexpired U.S. passport card.
- Qualifying foreign passport and immigration documents.

### Residency or citizenship documents

- Permanent Resident Card (Form I-551).
- Conditional Permanent Resident Card.
- Certificate of Naturalization.
- Certificate of Citizenship.

Check the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet's current document list to confirm which documents apply to your situation.

## Gather proof of Social Security number

The Kentucky Driver Licensing handout lists the following documents:

- Social Security card displaying your current name.
- Social Security Number Application Process (SSNAP) replacement printout.
- Most recently issued W-2 or 1099.
- Paycheck stub or statement.

Handwritten W-2 forms and pay stubs are not accepted.

First-time applicants must bring their Social Security card, according to the handout.

If your name has changed, update your Social Security record before applying to change the name on your Kentucky credential.

## Gather proof of Kentucky residency

For a REAL ID, bring two acceptable documents showing Kentucky residency.

For a standard credential, bring one acceptable document.

### Documents dated within the last year

The handout lists:

- Utility bill, such as electric, gas, or cable.
- Telephone bill.
- Bank statement.
- State or federal tax return.
- Pay slip or salary statement.
- Kentucky voter registration card.
- U.S. Postal Service postmarked mail.

### Other accepted documents

The handout also lists:

- Current Kentucky driver's license, permit, or identification card.
- Property tax bill.
- Kentucky vehicle registration.
- Recent W-2 or 1099.
- Lease or rental agreement.
- Mortgage statement.
- Insurance policy.
- Record from a Kentucky educational institution establishing enrollment.
- Kentucky titles.

A Kentucky post office box alone is not sufficient proof of a physical residential address.

## Change your name on your Kentucky ID

If your legal name differs from the name shown on your identification or lawful-status documents, you must provide legal proof of the change.

The Kentucky Driver Licensing handout identifies the following documents:

- Certified marriage certificate.
- Certified divorce decree showing that a name change was granted.
- Certified legal name-change order.
- Death certificate of spouse.
- Valid military identification.
- Updated Social Security card.

Bring all documents applicable to your name-change history.

### Update your Social Security record

Kentucky's current licensing instructions require you to update your name with the Social Security Administration before changing the name on your Kentucky driver's license or permit.

### Visit a Driver Licensing Regional Office

Kentucky requires legal name changes on driver's licenses and permits to be completed in person.

Bring your current credential, legal name-change documentation, and any other documents required for your application.

## Change your gender marker

The Kentucky Driver Licensing handout lists the following documents for gender-marker changes:

- Court order changing the gender marker.
- Letter from a surgeon verifying completion of gender reassignment.
- Amended birth certificate.

The handout identifies one of these documents as required.

The Kentucky Youth Law Project brochure also discusses gender-marker changes on state and federal identification documents.

**Important:** Gender-marker requirements may change because of legislation, court decisions, or agency policies. Confirm the current requirements directly with Kentucky Driver Licensing before applying.

## Visit a Driver Licensing Regional Office

Kentucky REAL ID applications must be completed in person at a Driver Licensing Regional Office.

Name and gender-marker changes to existing REAL IDs also require an in-person visit.

Before visiting:

1. Review the current list of acceptable documents.
2. Gather your identity, Social Security, and residency documents.
3. Bring legal documentation supporting any requested changes.
4. Check the current office procedures and applicable fees.

Use the [Kentucky IDocument Guide](https://drive.ky.gov/Pages/IDocument-Guide.aspx) to review the documents needed for your application.

## Receive your updated ID

Kentucky's REAL ID instructions state that applicants receive a temporary document after completing their application at a Driver Licensing Regional Office.

The permanent REAL ID is mailed to the address provided during the application process.

The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet currently estimates delivery within 10 to 15 business days for REAL ID credentials.

Confirm the current delivery timeframe with the licensing office.
