I am thrilled to share that Casey Bohannon, a long-time name change coordinator, union organizer, and systems builder, is joining Namesake!

Casey comes to Namesake following nearly 8 years at Advocates for Trans Equality (formerly the Trangender Legal Defense and Education Fund), where they helped double the program's yearly capacity and coordinate the referral of over 2,500 name changes.

They were a founding member of the first transgender union in the country (Union for Trans Liberation), and they served as the Union Chapter Chair for 6 years. Casey currently serves on the Philadelphia Mayor Cherelle Parker’s LGBTQ+ Commission, where they advocate for trans people at the city level.

![Casey Bohannon and Ky Decker pose and smile for a photo in front of a brick wall.](casey.png)

Since 2021, Namesake has been building technology to make the name change process easier. We have assisted over 1,000 trans people in [Massachusetts](https://namesake.fyi/guides/ma) and [Rhode Island](https://namesake.fyi/guides/ri) with name and gender marker changes, and we are working hard to expand support in New York and other states soon.

Non-profit funding has come under strain in recent years, forcing many organizations to tighten their budgets. Name change assistance is often deprioritized, particularly within larger organizations. Community surveys consistently report that access to safe identity documents is one of the most critical needs for trans people around the country. There is still much to do to improve [identity document laws and policies](https://mapresearch.org/equality-map/identity-document-laws-and-policies/#drivers-license) in the U.S.

## What's next for Namesake

In Casey's words:

> I'm excited to build on the amazing organizational foundation and technology that Ky, Luke and the whole team at Namesake have created over the last five years. Direct services to assist transgender people with the name change process are often the first programs to be cut when funding is tightened for LGBTQ+ organizations. We are prioritizing direct services to address national gaps. We are building a network of name change practitioners to triage and promptly reallocate resources across all fifty states, ensuring trans folks’ ID documents are most representative of them. 
>
> <cite>Casey Bohannon</cite>

Casey will be leading Namesake’s day-to-day operations and strategic planning, and I couldn't be happier to welcome them aboard.

We are focused on dramatically expanding Namesake support in 2027. If you run a name change project, clinic, or are a name change practitioner, we want to hear from you! Send us an email at [hey@namesake.fyi](mailto:hey@namesake.fyi).
